Welcome to Legend of Kairi

Legend of Kairi is built on your PC from your own copies of the games: it reads them, makes the mod's files and checks every one. It takes about 15 minutes. Your games stay as they are.

Your games
Set up

Everything the installer writes goes into one folder:

The folder you unpacked Savage Wind into. Start it once and set up your game files first: the installer reads them from there.

Your KINGDOM HEARTS HD 1.5+2.5 ReMIX folder from Steam. Sora, his forms, the party, the effects and the sounds are all made from it. It is found through Steam when it can be.

Builds the mod's files from your games, then checks each one against the reference build, so everyone who plays together has the very same files.

Who made this, and the free tools it is built with.

Installing Legend of Kairi

You can keep using your PC. If you close this window the install stops, and the next time it carries on where it stopped.

    Play Legend of Kairi – plays on your own. Start it from the folder below (or make a shortcut to it).

    Play Legend of Kairi Multiplayer – plays with friends. The first time, right-click it, choose Edit, and put your friend's Tailscale address on the marked line (for example 100.101.102.103:7790). Everybody needs the very same Legend of Kairi release, and Windows may ask to let the game through the firewall (UDP port 7790): say yes.
