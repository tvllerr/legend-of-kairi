Welcome to Legend of Kairi

Legend of Kairi is built on your PC from your own copies of the games: it reads them, makes the mod's files and checks every one. It takes about 15 minutes. Your games stay as they are.

Your games
Set up

Everything the installer writes goes into one folder:

The folder you unpacked Savage Wind into. Start it once and set up your game files first: the installer reads them from there. Still on Savage Wind 0.4.1 from Legend of Kairi 0.1.4? Choose that folder: the installer brings it up to right there and keeps your game files, saves and settings. (This release plays on your own. For co-op, stay on Legend of Kairi 0.1.4 with Savage Wind 0.4.1.)

Your KINGDOM HEARTS HD 1.5+2.5 ReMIX folder from Steam. Sora, his forms, the party, the effects and the sounds are all made from it. It is found through Steam when it can be.

Builds the mod's files from your games, then checks each one against the reference build, so everyone who plays together has the very same files.

Already playing an older Legend of Kairi? Choose its folder (the one with Play Legend of Kairi.bat) and it is brought up to right there: only what changed is rebuilt, so it takes a few minutes instead of 15. Your saves stay. A Savage Wind 0.4.1 folder is brought up to first.
